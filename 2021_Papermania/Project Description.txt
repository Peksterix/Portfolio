Game: Papermania
Project Duration: October 2021 - February 2022

Project Description:
This was my Final Year Project is a Online 3D Party Video Game that was developed on Unity Engine in collaboration with Trident Computer College from Japan during my time at Nanyang Polytechnic. My team consisted of 2 artists and 2 other programmers from Trident.

The game consists of 3 minigames, 1 programmer to each minigame. The minigame I worked on was King of the Hill where players are to fight off other players while attempting to stay on the highest platform the longest to gain points.

Other areas I worked on:
- Networking
- Menus
- Player mechanics
