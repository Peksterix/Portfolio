Hello and welcome to Dysbarism, a game of deep-sea exploration with a sonar and physics-based combat.

“How To Play”
Entering the game, you'll be introduced to the overworld scene, where you explore using the sonar.
Nodes will appear, which can be a city, wreckage, supplies which drop fuel or money, or even an enemy!
Keep an eye though, some of these nodes aren't what they seem to be...
Be sure to keep track of your fuel and collect money enough to repair and upgrade your ship.
When in combat, select your weapons, dodge and fire away!
Visit all 4 cities and the FINAL city to beat the game.

“Controls”
- In the Overworld:
	- RIGHT CLICK on the area you wish to move to
	- RIGHT CLICK to interact with nodes
	- SPACE to ping the area around you
	- Escape to return to main menu
- In Combat:
	- 1, 2, 3, 4 to cycle the respective gun port to mouse button
		- When unlit, it is bounded to nothing
		- When lit red, it is bounded to the LEFT MOUSE BUTTON
		- When lit blue, it is bounded to the RIGHT MOUSE BUTTON
	- LEFT CLICK or RIGHT CLICK to shoot
	- W, A, S, D to move in the 4 directions
	- Q, E to rotate clockwise/anti-clockwise
	- Escape to return to main menu
- In Credits:
	- LEFT CLICK to speed up the credits

Cheat codes
- In the Overworld
	- F1 for super speed
	- F2 for max money
	- F3 for max fuel
	- F5 to display current coordinates
	- F6 to teleport to the next goal city
- In Combat:
	- F1 to end combat
	- F2 to activate invulnerability
